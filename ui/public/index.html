Triage rules

Default policy. Exports may use custom rules; this viewer does not recalculate results.

Priority: P0 first, then P1, then P2

Priority sets queue order, not severity. All priority rules are evaluated; highest priority wins. Explicit P0/P1 is preserved.

  • P0: Explicit P0, trusted security fix, accepted link to P0, or low-effort community PR.
  • P1: Explicit P1, accepted link to P1, community PR, or an unprioritized PR older than 14 days.
  • P2: No higher rule matched.

Evidence requirements

  • Eligible PR: Open, unmerged, and confirmed to close the issue or associated by review. Historical PRs do not trigger active rules.
  • Community: Author verified outside configured Microsoft organizations; excluded Copilot identities do not qualify. Unknown membership is not proof.
  • Low effort: At most 5 files and 100 added/deleted lines; complete diff, no binary or omitted patches, added/modified tests, successful current-head tests and required checks, non-draft, and confirmed mergeability. Failed, pending, cancelled, unknown, or stale checks block this rule.
  • Overdue: PR created strictly more than 14 days before the UTC snapshot. Neither issue nor PR has explicit priority; unmapped priority-like labels block this rule.
  • Linked priority: Direct dependencies, shared confirmed closing PRs, or reviewed relationships. Use explicit priorities, not recursively derived ones or plain URL mentions.
  • Security: Trusted labels or reviewed evidence; keywords alone do not assign P0.

Verdict: first matching rule wins

Choose one active PR by highest matched priority, merge readiness, creation time, repository, then PR number. Evaluate that PR's blockers.

  1. security-review — Route a trusted fix through the security process, even during partial collection.
  2. data-incomplete — Retry required collection failures or restore access.
  3. ci-fix-needed — Repair failed current-head CI.
  4. awaiting-ci — Wait for pending/running current-head CI.
  5. pr-update-needed — Resolve draft status, conflicts, or requested changes.
  6. verify-release — Verify a merged fix with no active replacement PR.
  7. merge-candidate — Maintainer review of a low-effort community fix; never automatic merging.
  8. review-overdue — Prioritize and review the overdue unprioritized PR.
  9. review-community-pr — Review the community contribution.
  10. linked-priority-review — Review the linked P0/P1 dependency or shared fix.
  11. needs-information — Request missing evidence identified by review or configured labels.
  12. triage-needed — Fallback: diagnose and confirm ownership.
